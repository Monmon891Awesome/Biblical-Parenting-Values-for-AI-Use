# Translations and Copyright Notes

This repository studies Scripture primarily in two English translations, deliberately chosen to complement each other.

## Why KJV and NASB1995

| | KJV (1611/1769) | NASB1995 |
|---|---|---|
| Translation philosophy | Formal equivalence | Formal equivalence (often considered the most literal major modern translation) |
| Textual basis (NT) | Textus Receptus | Nestle-Aland / UBS critical text |
| Strengths for this project | Public domain; centuries of devotional and theological heritage; memorability | Precision for word study; transparent rendering of Hebrew/Greek syntax; capitalizes pronouns referring to God; small caps for OT quotations in the NT |

Reading both side by side surfaces translation decisions (e.g., "nurture and admonition" vs. "discipline and instruction" in Ephesians 6:4) that push us back to the Hebrew and Greek — which is exactly the study method this repo teaches (see [`research/00-methodology.md`](../research/00-methodology.md)).

## Copyright

### KJV
The Authorized (King James) Version is in the **public domain** in most of the world (Crown patent applies in the UK). Quotation here is unrestricted.

### NASB1995
The New American Standard Bible is copyrighted by The Lockman Foundation. Quotations in this repository follow the Lockman Foundation's gratis use guidelines (limited verse counts, not a complete book, less than the majority of any work quoting it) and carry this notice:

> Scripture quotations taken from the (NASB®) New American Standard Bible®, Copyright © 1960, 1971, 1977, 1995 by The Lockman Foundation. Used by permission. All rights reserved. [lockman.org](https://www.lockman.org)

**Rule for contributors and for any app built from this repo:** keep NASB1995 quotations selective (individual verses and short passages with attribution). For full-chapter display inside the future app, either license the NASB properly through the Lockman Foundation / a Bible API provider (e.g., API.Bible, Biblia) or fall back to public-domain text (KJV, WEB) for bulk display.

### Hebrew/Greek data
Strong's numbers, lexicon glosses, and etymological notes summarized in the word studies are drawn from public-domain sources (Strong's Exhaustive Concordance; Brown–Driver–Briggs; Thayer's Greek Lexicon) with occasional reference to standard scholarly consensus. Original-language text (BHS/NA28 readings) is quoted only in short fragments for study purposes.

## Quotation style used across the repo

- Verses are labeled `(KJV)` or `(NASB1995)`.
- NASB small-caps OT quotations are rendered in normal case with a footnote where relevant.
- Divine name: `LORD` (small caps in print) is written as "LORD" for *YHWH* in both translations.
