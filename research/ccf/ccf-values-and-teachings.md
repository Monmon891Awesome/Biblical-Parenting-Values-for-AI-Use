# CCF — Christ's Commission Fellowship: Vision, Mission, Values, and Family Teachings

CCF (Christ's Commission Fellowship) is a church-planting and discipleship movement founded in the Philippines in 1984 by Dr. Peter Tan-Chi, named for the Great Commission (Matthew 28:18–20). Its discipleship DNA — especially its family teaching — is a living, modern expression of the Deuteronomy 6 pattern this repo studies, which is why it anchors the "church stream" of our research.

> Sources: [CCF — Who We Are](https://www.ccf.org.ph/who-we-are/), [GLC faculty — Dr. Peter & Deonna Tan-Chi](https://glc.ccf.org.ph/faculty/peter-tan-chi/), [MOTIVATE (book)](https://books.google.com/books/about/M_O_T_I_V_A_T_E_Updated_Edition.html?id=oU_6DwAAQBAJ), [Salt&Light interview](https://saltandlight.sg/family/if-you-do-not-have-their-hearts-you-will-not-have-their-minds-rev-dr-peter-tan-chi-on-how-to-disciple-our-children-well/). ## Mission

> **"To honor God and make Christ-committed followers who will make Christ-committed followers."**

Note the multiplication built into the sentence — disciples who make disciples (2 Timothy 2:2). Parenting, in CCF teaching, is the first and nearest field of this mission: your children are your first disciples.

## Vision

> A movement of millions of committed followers of the Lord Jesus Christ, meeting in small groups, transforming lives, families, communities, and nations for the glory of God.

Families are explicitly in the vision statement. Transformation scales *outward from the household*.

## Core value themes (as taught in CCF discipleship)

CCF's discipleship culture consistently emphasizes:

1. **Consistent personal walk** — daily devotions, prayer, personal study of God's Word (the believer's own time with God comes first).
2. **Love that is visible** — including for difficult people; guarding relationships; no gossip or critical spirit (Ephesians 4:29–32).
3. **Integrity in private life** — purity and freedom from addictions; the private person matches the public one (Psalm 51:6).
4. **Word-driven decisions** — applying Scripture to everyday choices and priorities (Proverbs 3:5–6).
5. **Respect for God-given authority** — submission with a positive spirit (Romans 13:1; Hebrews 13:17).
6. **Evangelism and discipleship as lifestyle** — consistently sharing the gospel and walking with others (Matthew 28:19–20).

These function like a modern *musar* list — practical covenant wisdom — and map almost one-to-one onto the AI values in [`../../ai-values/VALUES.md`](../../ai-values/VALUES.md).

## Dgroups — discipleship groups

CCF's core method: small groups (Dgroups) where believers walk together through the Word, accountability, and mission. For this repo, Dgroups matter for two reasons:

1. **They are the anti-isolation structure.** A parent with a question should ordinarily bring it to their Dgroup and church elders — real people who know them — before (and after) any app. The skill in this repo *actively refers users to their small group and church*.
2. **They model multiplication in the home.** CCF language: your family is your first Dgroup.

## Dr. Peter & Deonna Tan-Chi — M.O.T.I.V.A.T.E.

Eight principles of parenting from their book *MOTIVATE: 8 Secrets of Successful Parenting*, each with the Biblical anchor this repo's research supplies:

| Letter | Principle | Biblical anchor |
|---|---|---|
| **M** | **Model** the Christian life | Deut 6:5–6; Luke 6:40; 1 Cor 11:1 |
| **O** | **Open** communication | Prov 18:13; James 1:19; Eph 6:4 (*nouthesia*) |
| **T** | **Time** — build relationship through time together | Deut 6:7 (sit/walk/lie/rise) |
| **I** | **Intimacy** — influence flows through closeness | Ps 103:13; 1 Thess 2:7–8 |
| **V** | **Vision** — give direction and purpose | Prov 29:18; Ps 127:4 (arrows aimed) |
| **A** | **Affirmation** | Prov 16:24; Matt 3:17 ("This is My beloved Son") |
| **T** | **Teach & Train** Biblical principles | Deut 6:7 (*shanan*); Eph 6:4 (*paideia*) |
| **E** | **Entrust** them to God | Ps 127:1,3; Phil 1:6 |

## The MRI principle — Model, Relationship, Intentionality

Tan-Chi's compact summary of family discipleship:

- **Model** — authentically live what you want your children to become.
- **Relationship** — consistent time together builds the bridge influence travels on. His most-quoted line: **"If you do not have their hearts, you will not have their minds."**
- **Intentionality** — discipleship by design, not drift.

## Heart-first discipleship

> "Who is supposed to disciple our children? Not the school, not even the church, not even the youth pastor. It's the parents." — Peter Tan-Chi

> "The heart is the root; behaviour is the fruit."

Both statements are direct restatements of Deuteronomy 6:6–7 and Proverbs 4:23 — which is exactly why this repo pairs CCF teaching with the original-language studies: the church stream and the text stream confirm each other.

## What the AI layer inherits from CCF

1. **Multiplication posture**: success = the user discipling others (starting at home), not the user returning to the app.
2. **Dgroup referral**: real community is the context for growth; the AI routinely asks, "Who in your church/small group have you talked to about this?"
3. **Devotions before advice**: like CCF's first core value, the skill asks about the user's own walk with God before dispensing parenting technique.
4. **Heart over behavior**: questions target the heart of parent and child, not surface compliance.
5. **MOTIVATE as a diagnostic**: the eight letters become a gentle checklist the skill can walk a parent through.
