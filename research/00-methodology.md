# Study Methodology

How every study in this repository is built — and the method the AI skill teaches users, so that *they* learn to study rather than letting AI study for them (Acts 17:11; 2 Timothy 2:15).

## 1. Parallel translation reading (KJV ∥ NASB1995)

Every passage study quotes the King James Version and the NASB1995 side by side.

- **Where they agree**, we have high confidence the English carries the original sense.
- **Where they differ** (e.g., KJV "nurture" vs. NASB "discipline" in Ephesians 6:4), the difference is a *doorway* into the Hebrew or Greek, not a problem. We open the doorway with a word study.

## 2. Word-origin (etymology + usage) method

For each key term we record:

1. **Lemma** — the Hebrew/Greek word with transliteration and Strong's number.
2. **Root and origin** — where the word comes from (e.g., Hebrew *chanak* "train/dedicate" is related to the word behind *Hanukkah*, "dedication").
3. **Range of usage** — where else Scripture uses it; usage defines meaning more than etymology does. (Etymology suggests; usage decides. We flag folk etymologies as such.)
4. **Both translations' rendering** — how KJV and NASB1995 handle it.
5. **Parenting/discipleship payoff** — what the word actually contributes to living the passage.

Sources: Strong's Concordance, Brown–Driver–Briggs (Hebrew), Thayer's (Greek) — all public domain — checked against standard modern consensus. See [`../docs/translations-and-copyright.md`](../docs/translations-and-copyright.md).

## 3. Context before application (grammatical-historical)

Consistent with Reformed/Baptist hermeneutics:

- **Scripture interprets Scripture** (the analogy of faith). An unclear text is read in the light of clear texts.
- **Genre matters.** Proverbs are covenant wisdom generalizations, not unconditional promises — Proverbs 22:6 describes the ordinary fruit of faithful training, while leaving room for God's sovereignty and the child's responsibility.
- **Christ is the center.** All Scripture ultimately testifies of Him (Luke 24:27; John 5:39). Parenting texts are read inside the gospel, not as a self-help program.

## 4. From text to values

Each study ends with **principles** (stated plainly) and **heart questions** (reflection, not homework). These feed the distilled value system in [`../ai-values/`](../ai-values/) and the chapter content in the [PRD](../prd/biblical-parenting-guide-app-prd.md).

## 5. Rules the AI must inherit from this method

1. Quote Scripture accurately, with translation labels — never paraphrase and call it quotation.
2. Distinguish **text** (what it says), **interpretation** (what it means), and **application** (what we do) — and say which one you're offering.
3. Where faithful Christians differ (e.g., specific discipline practices, schooling choices), present the Biblical principles and send the user to their own pastor, elders, spouse, and small group.
4. Always leave the user with something to *do before God* — a verse to read, a prayer to pray, a conversation to have — not merely something to know.
