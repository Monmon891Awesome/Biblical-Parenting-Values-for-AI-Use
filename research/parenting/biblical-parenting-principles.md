# Biblical Parenting Principles — The Synthesis

This file distills the whole research library into the principles that drive the AI values, the Claude skill, and the app's chapters. Every principle cites its research source.

## The ten principles

### 1. Worship precedes parenting
Love the LORD your God with all your heart — *then* teach your children (Deuteronomy 6:5–7). A parent's own walk with Christ is the first parenting intervention. *(Source: [Deuteronomy 6 study](../scripture-studies/deuteronomy-6-family-discipleship.md); CCF core value #1.)*

### 2. Parents are the primary disciplers
Not the school, not the church program, not the youth pastor — and not an AI (Deuteronomy 6:7; Psalm 78:5–7; Ephesians 6:4; Tan-Chi). Everyone else assists. *(Source: [CCF](../ccf/ccf-values-and-teachings.md); [Deut 6](../scripture-studies/deuteronomy-6-family-discipleship.md).)*

### 3. Children are gifts to receive and arrows to release
Heritage and reward (Psalm 127:3), aimed and sent (127:4–5), ultimately entrusted to God (MOTIVATE "E"). Neither idols nor projects. *(Source: [Psalms 127–128 study](../scripture-studies/psalms-127-128-children-a-heritage.md).)*

### 4. Aim at the heart, not the behavior
Foolishness is bound in the *heart* (Proverbs 22:15); guard the *heart* (4:23); the heart is the root, behavior the fruit (Tan-Chi). Techniques that only suppress symptoms fail the child. *(Source: [Proverbs study](../scripture-studies/proverbs-on-parenting.md); Hebrew *lev*.)*

### 5. Train and talk — both channels, always
*Paideia* (structured formation) plus *nouthesia* (heart-reaching words) — Ephesians 6:4; "rod and reproof" — Proverbs 29:15. Consequence without conversation hardens; conversation without follow-through spoils. *(Source: [Ephesians/Colossians study](../scripture-studies/ephesians-6-and-colossians-3.md); [Greek studies](../word-studies/greek-word-studies.md).)*

### 6. Discipline flows from delight
"Whom the LORD loveth he correcteth; even as a father the son in whom he delighteth" (Proverbs 3:12, KJV; Hebrews 12:5–11). Correction in rage is provocation, and provocation is forbidden (Ephesians 6:4; Colossians 3:21). Watch the child's spirit for "losing heart." *(Source: [Proverbs](../scripture-studies/proverbs-on-parenting.md) and [household codes](../scripture-studies/ephesians-6-and-colossians-3.md) studies.)*

### 7. Repetition in the rhythm of ordinary life
*Shanan* — whet, sharpen, repeat: sitting, walking, lying down, rising up (Deuteronomy 6:7). Family worship small and daily beats grand and rare. *(Source: [Hebrew studies](../word-studies/hebrew-word-studies.md); [family worship](../theology/family-worship-and-catechesis.md).)*

### 8. Model what you want multiplied
A fully trained disciple becomes like the teacher (Luke 6:40). Children inherit what parents *are*. MOTIVATE begins with Model; MRI begins with Model. *(Source: [CCF](../ccf/ccf-values-and-teachings.md); Greek *mathētēs*.)*

### 9. Words give life or death
Soft answers, honeycomb words, affirmation as the Father affirmed the Son (Proverbs 15:1; 16:24; 18:21; Matthew 3:17). The home's vocabulary is discipleship infrastructure. *(Source: [Proverbs study](../scripture-studies/proverbs-on-parenting.md).)*

### 10. God builds the house — so parents can rest
Unless the LORD builds, laborers labor in vain; He gives to His beloved sleep (Psalm 127:1–2). God saves children by grace, using parental faithfulness as means, not mechanism. Diligence without messianic pressure; failures met by mercies new every morning. *(Source: [Psalm 127](../scripture-studies/psalms-127-128-children-a-heritage.md); [Reformed foundations](../theology/reformed-baptist-foundations.md); [wellbeing](parental-wellbeing.md).)*

## The gospel thread through all ten

Parents cannot keep these principles perfectly — that is not a footnote; it *is* the gospel setup. Christ kept the whole law of love, died for parental failures too, and rose to send His Spirit (the true *Paraklētos*, John 14:16) into ordinary kitchens and minivans. Biblical parenting is therefore **grace-powered repentance-and-repair**, not performance: when you blow it, you model the most important lesson of all — how a sinner runs to Christ (1 John 1:9), including asking your child's forgiveness (James 5:16).

## Quick diagnostic (used by the skill)

When a parenting problem arrives, walk this ladder — top first:

1. **My walk** — How is my own time with God? (Principle 1)
2. **My model** — Is my child seeing this behavior in me? (Principle 8)
3. **The relationship** — Do I have my child's heart right now? (MRI; Principle 4)
4. **The channels** — Am I using both training and talk? (Principle 5)
5. **My tone** — Delight or rage? Any signs of a child losing heart? (Principle 6)
6. **The rhythm** — What do our ordinary days repeat? (Principle 7)
7. **The rest** — Have I actually prayed about this, or only worried? (Principle 10)
